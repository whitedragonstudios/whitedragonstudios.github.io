About the island

A small island with a big personality

Taniti is a small, tropical island in the Pacific. With an area of less than 500 square miles, it packs in an astonishing variety of terrain: sandy and rocky beaches, a small but safe harbor, lush tropical rainforests, and a mountainous interior crowned by a small, active volcano.

About 20,000 Tanitians call the island home. Until tourism began to grow, most of the local economy was built on fishing and agriculture, and you will still find the island's seafood and fresh produce on nearly every menu. Fishing boats leave the harbor before sunrise, and the morning catch often ends up on a dinner plate the very same day.

Life on Taniti moves at a gentle pace. Mornings begin early, afternoons slow down in the heat of the day, and evenings are for sharing meals, music and stories. Visitors who take the time to chat with shopkeepers, guides and hosts quickly discover how proud Tanitians are of their home, and how eager they are to help newcomers feel welcome.

Most visitors come for the beaches, the rainforest and the volcano, but there is plenty more to explore, from art galleries and a local history museum to snorkeling, zip-lining and a brand-new dance club. Many of those activities are clustered around Merriton Landing, while Taniti City offers native architecture and easy access to the white sands of Yellow Leaf Bay.

Tourism is growing quickly, and the island is growing with it. A larger airport, new restaurants and a nine-hole golf course are all on the way. Fast facts

Currency
U.S. dollar. Many businesses also take euros and yen, and several banks exchange currency. Major credit cards are widely accepted.
Power
120 volts, the same as in the United States.
Language
Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English. Very little is spoken in rural areas, especially by older residents.
Alcohol
Not served or sold from midnight to 9:00 a.m. The drinking age is 18, though it is not strictly enforced.
Health & safety
One hospital (with many multilingual staff) and several clinics. Violent crime is very rare, but watch for pickpockets.
Holidays
Taniti has many national holidays, and attractions and restaurants may close, so plan ahead.
