What to do

Beaches, rainforest and a volcano

Most people visit Taniti to enjoy the beaches, explore the rainforest and visit the volcano. Most visitors spend much of their time in Taniti City, where native architecture meets the white, sandy beaches that circle Yellow Leaf Bay. Boat and bus tours of the island and rainforest hikes are also popular.

There is plenty more to discover: a local history museum, chartered fishing tours, snorkeling, zip-lining, pubs (including a microbrewery), a new dance club, a movie theater, helicopter rides, an arcade, art galleries and bowling. Many of these are found in Merriton Landing, a rapidly developing area on the north side of Yellow Leaf Bay.

Plan your days around the weather and your energy. Mornings are cool and clear, which makes them ideal for hiking and volcano tours. Afternoons are for the beach, the lagoon or a long lunch in the shade, and evenings come alive with music, pubs and the new dance club.

Taniti has a large number of national holidays, and many attractions and restaurants close on those days, so check ahead. A nine-hole golf course should be up and running by next year. Pick a category below to start planning.

Beaches

Beaches information

Taniti has both sandy and rocky beaches. Whether you want a lively stretch of sand or a quiet cove, there is a spot for you.

Pack reef-safe sunscreen, a hat and plenty of water. Lifeguards are posted at the most popular beaches, and local signs mark areas where currents can be strong.

Each beach has its own personality. The north shore is wild and windswept, the lagoon is calm and glassy, and Yellow Leaf Bay is lively and sociable. If you only have a day, try a quiet sunrise on the north shore, snorkel the lagoon in the late morning and spend the afternoon at the bay.

Good to know

  • Wear reef-safe sunscreen to protect the coral.
  • Check posted signs for currents before swimming.
  • Bring water and shade, since some beaches have few facilities.

North shore

The north shore has a dramatic coastline with a mix of sandy and rocky beaches, perfect for sunrise walks and tide pooling. Waves are bigger here, so it is better for watching than swimming on windy days.

Adventure

Adventure information

Climb, zip and fly your way around the island. Taniti's volcano, rainforest and open sky make it a playground for adventurers of every level.

Book popular tours a day or two ahead, wear sturdy shoes and bring a rain jacket for the forest. When you want a break from the outdoors, Merriton Landing's arcade is a playful alternative.

Adventure on Taniti is surprisingly varied. In the morning you can hike to the rim of an active volcano, by midday glide through the canopy on a zipline, and by evening be tossing tokens into a game at the arcade. Most tours are small and led by guides who grew up on the island.

Good to know

  • Book volcano and zipline tours a day or two ahead.
  • Wear closed-toe shoes and bring a rain jacket.
  • Check weather and trail conditions with your guide.

Volcano Tours

Guided visits to Taniti's small, active volcano go by foot, bus or helicopter, with stunning views from the rim. Guides explain how the volcano shaped the island and keep groups on safe trails.

Shopping

Shopping information

Browse art galleries, island crafts and everyday essentials. Taniti has two supermarkets, two smaller grocery stores and a convenience store that is open 24 hours a day.

Look for handmade jewelry, bright island clothing and local art. Many shops accept major credit cards, and some also take euros and yen, but small stalls may prefer cash.

Shopping on Taniti is part treasure hunt and part social visit. Shopkeepers love to talk about where their goods come from, and many pieces are made by local artists. Do not forget practical stops too: the two supermarkets and the 24-hour convenience store keep you stocked at any hour.

Good to know

  • Many shops close on national holidays, so plan ahead.
  • Major credit cards are accepted widely, but carry some cash.
  • U.S. dollars are the main currency, with euros and yen accepted by some.

Dining

Dining information

Taniti has ten restaurants: five serve mostly local fish and rice, three serve American-style meals and two serve Pan-Asian cuisine.

Reservations are a good idea on weekends. Many restaurants close on national holidays, so check ahead and keep the 24-hour convenience store in mind for a late snack. Alcohol is not served between midnight and 9:00 a.m.

Dining is a highlight of any Tanitian day. Start with a plate of fresh local fish and rice, try an American-style classic when you want something familiar, and save an evening for one of the two Pan-Asian restaurants. Pair meals with island fruit juices, and remember that alcohol is not served between midnight and 9:00 a.m.

Good to know

  • Reserve tables on weekends and holidays.
  • Ask about the catch of the day at local fish restaurants.
  • The drinking age is 18, though it is not strictly enforced.
