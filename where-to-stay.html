Where to stay

Find your home in paradise

Taniti has a wide variety of places to stay, from an inexpensive hostel to one large, four-star resort. In between you will find many small, family-owned hotels and a growing number of bed and breakfasts, plus beach houses and condo rentals for longer stays.

Every type of lodging on the island is strictly regulated and regularly inspected by the Tanitian government. That means you can book with confidence, knowing that safety, cleanliness and fair pricing are checked on a regular schedule. If you ever have a concern, your host or the front desk can point you to the right office.

Location matters as much as the room. Many visitors choose to stay around Merriton Landing, a rapidly developing area on the north side of Yellow Leaf Bay that is easy to explore on foot, with restaurants, pubs, a cinema and an arcade nearby. Others prefer the native architecture and sandy beaches of Taniti City, or a quiet beach house along the coast.

Whatever you choose, book early for the busy season and ask your host about airport pickup, breakfast and the best sunset views. Browse the lodging types below to find the right fit for your trip.

Hotels

Hotel information

From the island's one large four-star resort to friendly, family-owned hotels, there is a room for every budget. An inexpensive hostel is also available for backpackers and solo travelers.

Hotels are a great choice for short stays because breakfast, housekeeping and local advice are all close at hand. Many are within walking distance of the beach or the waterfront.

When comparing hotels, think about how you want to spend your days. A hotel near Merriton Landing puts you within walking distance of nightlife and dining, while one in Taniti City keeps you close to the beaches and native architecture. The large four-star resort has the most amenities, while the smaller family-owned hotels offer the most personality.

Good to know

  • All lodging is inspected regularly by the Tanitian government.
  • Ask whether breakfast and airport pickup are included.
  • Book early for national holidays and the dry season.

Beach House

Beach house information

Wake up to the sound of the surf. Beach houses are ideal for families and groups who want space, a kitchen and sand just outside the door.

Most beach houses include outdoor showers, hammocks and a shaded porch. Shops and markets are a short drive away, so plan a first grocery run when you arrive.

A beach house gives you room to spread out. Kids can run between the porch and the water, adults can cook together in a real kitchen, and everyone can share a hammock at sunset. It is the best option for family reunions, friend trips and anyone who likes to wake up to waves.

Good to know

  • Stock up on groceries at a supermarket on your first day.
  • Ask about outdoor showers, hammocks and beach gear.
  • Keep valuables locked up, since petty theft is on the rise.

Condo Rental

Condo rental information

Furnished condos are a smart choice for longer stays, with kitchens, laundry and a home-away-from-home feel close to Merriton Landing.

Many condos have balconies with views of the bay or the mountains. They are perfect for groups, remote workers and families who want to cook a few meals with fresh local produce.

Condos make longer visits comfortable. Having a washing machine, a full kitchen and a balcony changes the feel of a trip from vacation to temporary island life. Many condos are near Merriton Landing, so you can walk to dinner, then stroll back along the water.

Good to know

  • Confirm the cleaning fee and check-in process before booking.
  • Ask about weekly or monthly discounts for stays of a week or more.
  • Check that the condo has air conditioning and reliable Wi-Fi.

Bed and Breakfast

Bed and Breakfast information

A growing number of bed and breakfasts offer personal service, home-cooked island breakfasts and insider tips from local hosts.

These small, family-run homes are a wonderful way to meet Tanitians. Expect a handful of rooms, a shared dining table and a host who knows the best place for fresh fish and rice.

Staying at a bed and breakfast is the closest thing to staying with a Tanitian family. Hosts share stories about the island, recommend their favorite fishing spots and often invite guests to join a neighborhood event. Rooms are few, so these places fill up quickly.

Good to know

  • Reserve early, since B&Bs only have a handful of rooms.
  • Mention any food allergies when you book breakfast.
  • Ask your host for local restaurant and beach tips.
